How do I add staves to the page?


Choose the Tracks>Insert Track command to insert a new track into the score. When you choose the Insert Track command, the Insert Instrument dialog box opens.


  1. Click on the track where you want to insert the new track.
  2. Choose Tracks>Insert Track to open the Insert Track dialog box.
  3. Choose the playback device and instrument you want to insert.
  4. Check the Insert below current track button if you wish to insert the track below the current track.
  5. Click the Done button.
