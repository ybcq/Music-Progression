Offline Activation

If you wish to activate Overture on a computer that is not connected to the Internet or is having problems connecting to our authorization server, then use the following process.


When Overture starts and has not been activated you will see the following dialog.



Press the Activate Offline button to start the offline activation process.


The following dialog will appear.



There is a two part process to activating Overture off line.


Part 1

  1. Enter your email address that you used at the time of purchase.
  2. Enter your name that you used at the time of purchase.
  3. Enter the serial number found in the email that was sent to you after the purchase.

    It will look like this on your emailed receipt.


Serial Keys


    • Overture 5      XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX


Note: You can also find this number by logging into your account at www.sonicscores.com

You'll see the following:


Serial Keys

    • Overture 5 (Product Type: PC Download)      XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX


  1. Copy the serial number from your receipt onto the clipboard. (Avoid trying to type it the long number because it increases the chance of error).
  2. Paste the copied serial number into the Serial Number field.
  3. Once you enter all the data, the Save challenge button will be enabled.
  4. Press the Save challenge button and you will be prompted with a Save dialog.
  5. Press the Save button to save the file onto your desktop.
  6. Email this file to sales@sonicscores.com


Part 2

After you receive the "response file" from Sonic Scores do the following:


  1. Save the file from your email to the desktop.

    As above,
  2. Enter your email address that you used at the time of purchase.
  3. Enter your name that you used at the time of purchase.
  4. Enter the serial number found in the email that sent to you after the purchase.
  5. Copy the serial number from your receipt onto the clipboard.  (Avoid trying to type it the long number because it increases the chance of error).
  6. Paste the number into the Serial Number field.
  7. Once you enter in all the data, the Load response button will be enabled.
  8. Press the Load response button and you will be prompted with an Open dialog.
  9. Browse to the response file, if needed, and then press the Open button.


If everything has been entered correctly the dialog will disappear and Overture will be activated.

If not an error message will appear. Make sure all data is entered correctly and try again.

If it still fails email the message to sales@sonicscores.com with the error message.


Good luck and we hope you enjoy the worlds best music composition tool.
