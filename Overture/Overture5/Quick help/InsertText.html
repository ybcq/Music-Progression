Insert Text

Getting Started ›› Entering symbols ››

If Tools Panel is not showing click on the "Score Tools" button at top left of Control Bar or type  "Shift-T" to open the panel and then click on the Text section in the header if it's not active. You can also type "Ctrl[cmd]-Shift-T" to go directly to the Text section.


  1. Select the Text type from the four choices. If the text type is Expression, then click on the expression text to enter.
  2. Click on the score to enter the text. If the text was not an expression a text dialog appears for you to enter the text and the it's style.


Note: You can drag and drop expressions onto the score.
