As Recorded

Overture Menus ›› Options Menu ›› Play Style ››

Options>Play Style>As Recorded


Select this option if you want Overture to play back your score exactly as recorded. When you select As Recorded, Overture plays back any timing nuances exactly as your recorded them. It also faithfully plays any changes you may have made using the Edit MIDI Data mode.
