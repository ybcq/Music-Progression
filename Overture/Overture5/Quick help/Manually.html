Notes>Beam>Manually


Choose this command or press Ctrl[Cmd]-shift-b to beam all selected notes as a single group regardless of the meter of the measure(s). This command beams over any selected rests and across barlines if requested. This command overrides previous beam commands, including the Options>AutoBeam toggle.
