Pencil Button


Click the Pencil Tool button to select the Insertion tool. Use the Insertion tool to insert symbols that have been selected in the Score Tools panels.

Clicking this tool puts Overture into Insert mode.


Click the down arrow at the bottom right of the Pencil button to choose alternate insertion tools from a pop-up menu.


You can exit out of insert mode at anytime by typing the esc key to activate the Selection tool (unless you're in one of Overture's text entering modes).
