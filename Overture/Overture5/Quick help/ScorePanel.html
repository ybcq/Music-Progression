Score Layout Panel - Score Panel


If the Score Layout panel is not showing click on the Layout button at top right of Control Bar or type "Shift-L" to open the panel.

Then choose the Score panel.


The Score Panel is where you set the overall appearance of .

Tabs - allows you to choose what to edit.

  • Style - The general appearance of your score.
  • Font Map - Allows you to remap music symbols to symbols in another font.
  • Time Code - SMPTE time code settings.
  • Background - The Score's background image.
