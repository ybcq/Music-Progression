Measures>Breaks>Lock


Choose this command to lock a measure. After this command you will see a small unlock icon at the top right of the measure. When you have manually spaced notes in a measure, it is sometimes useful to exclude that measure from being affected by a the Justify or Recalc Layout command.


Note: To unlock the measure use the Measures>Breaks>Unlock command.
