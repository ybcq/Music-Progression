Using Brackets nd Braces

Symbol Palettes ›› Groups Palette ››

Use brackets and braces to enclose a group of notes.


To enter and edit a horizontal bracket:


  1. Choose the Bracket tool from the Groups palette,
  2. Drag over the notes you wish to group and release the mouse.


 to create  


  1. Drag the control points to change the slope and/or length of the bracket.


To enter and edit a vertical brace or bracket:


  1. Choose the Brace or Bracket tool from the Groups palette,
  2. Drag over the notes you wish to group and release the mouse.
