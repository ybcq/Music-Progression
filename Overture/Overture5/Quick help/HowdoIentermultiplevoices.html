How do I enter multiple voices?

Frequently Asked Questions ››

A voice is a single melodic line in a track. Each track may have up to four voices. For further explanation of voices, consult Terms.


For example lets enter two quarter notes in voice 1 and a half note in voice two:


  1. Choose the quarter note in the notes palette.
  2. Click in the score twice to enter two quarter notes.
  3. Press the esc key to deselect the last note entered.
  4. Choose the half note in the note palette.
  5. Select Voice 2 from the View Menu at the top right of the control bar or type Ctrl (Command) 2.
    Selecting Voice 2 dims the notes belonging to Voice 1:
  6. Click below the two quarter notes.
