Track Inspector Panel


If the Inspector panel is not showing click on the Inspector button at top right of Control Bar or type "Shift-I" to open the panel.


The Inspector panel contains settings for the appearance and playback of the current track.

Tabs - Choose what to edit

  • Track - General track settings
  • Articulations - playback settings for track articulations
  • Expression - playback settings for track expressions
