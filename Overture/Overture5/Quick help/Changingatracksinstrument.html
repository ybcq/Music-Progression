Changing a track's instrument

All about Tracks ››

To open the Instrument Library panel, click on the Library view button or type Shift+Y



To change an existing track's device:

  1. Set the options at the top to determine how the new track will be created.
  2. Choose the device (MIDI or Software Instrument) device for the new track.
  3. From the Instruments tree list, choose the instrument.
  4. Drag and drop the instrument onto your the track to be changed.
