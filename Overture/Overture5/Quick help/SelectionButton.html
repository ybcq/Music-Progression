Selection Tool Button


Click the Selection Tool button to select the Selection tool. Use the Selection tool to select or drag musical symbols, staves and text.

You can type the esc key to activate the Selection tool (unless you're in one of Overture's text entering modes).


Click the down arrow at the bottom right of the Selection button to choose alternate tools from a pop-up menu.
