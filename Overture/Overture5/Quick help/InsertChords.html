Insert Chords

Getting Started ›› Entering symbols ››

If Tools Panel is not showing click on the "Score Tools" button at top left of Control Bar or type "Shift-T" to open the panel and then on the Chords section if it's not active. You can also type "Ctrl[cmd]-Shift-C" to go directly to the Chord section.


Note: If you only want to display the chord name, click on the Settings tab, and deselect "Show Guitar Frames" check box.


There are three methods of entering chords:


Using mouse:

  1. Use the Root, Bass, Suffix, and Tuning to choose the chord type.
  2. Click on the desired guitar frame and then click on the score.
    You may optionally click and drag the guitar frame onto the score.


Using computer keyboard:

  1. Click on the score at the desired location.
  2. A small text entry box appears.
  3. Type in the actual chord phrase, such as "Cm7".
  4. Type space to enter the chord and move to next position. You can set the position interval using the values at top of panel. Press return to enter the chord and exit chord entry. Press esc to exit at anytime.


Using MIDI keyboard:

  1. Click on the score at the desired location.
  2. A small text entry box appears.
  3. Play the actual chord using your MIDI keyboard.
