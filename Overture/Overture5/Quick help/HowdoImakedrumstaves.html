How do I make drum staves?


You can make any staff in the score into a drum or percussion staff using the Track Inspector Panel.


To change a staff into a percussion staff:


  1. Click the staff you want to change and choose open the Track Inspector.
  2. Choose the Settings Tab
  3. Click on the Percussion Staff radio button
    This makes the current staff a percussion staff.


To set how notes are displayed on a percussion staff:


  1. Click on the Drum Tab
  2. A chart appears containing MIDI pitches, drum names, staff position and note heads.
    From then on, you can add notes to the staves easily as percussion notes.
    Percussion notes have an scale position, an MIDI pitch, and note head.
  3. To change a click on a note's scale position click on the note head and drag it to a new staff position.
  4. To change a note's pitch, click on the note and choose the pitch from the Pitch menu.
  5. To change a note's head set, click on the note and choose the head from the Head mens.


Whenever you record on that track using MIDI or insert notes, the MIDI pitches you specify appear in the staff positions you choose with the note head you want. For more information on percussion and drum notation, see Note heads.
