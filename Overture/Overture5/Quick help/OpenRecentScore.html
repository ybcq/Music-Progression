Open Recent Score

Getting Started ››


  1. Choose which scores by date using button on left side of dialog.
  2. Choose the recent score to open.
  3. Click on the desired score in the list and click the Done button or just double-click on the score.


Note: You can remove any score from the list by clicking on the small x at right side of score name.
