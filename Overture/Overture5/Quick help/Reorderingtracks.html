Reordering tracks

All about Tracks ››

There are two ways to reorder tracks.


  1. When working in the Score View choose the Hand Tool from the pull down menu under the Arrow Tool button.
    Click on any track and drag it to the desired new position.
  2. When working in the Track List View click on the track number and drag the track to the desired new position.
