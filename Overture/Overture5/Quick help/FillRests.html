Measures>Fill Rests


When you choose this command, rests are inserted in place of missing beats in the active measure. The active measure is the one that contains the insertion point.

To fill a measure with rests:


  1. Click in the measure to make it the active measure.
  2. Choose Measures>Fill in Rests.
    Overture fills in the measure with the necessary rests.


This is also a quick way to insert rests into any blank measures.
