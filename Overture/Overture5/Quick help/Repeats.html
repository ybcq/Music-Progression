Repeats

Overture Menus ›› Options Menu ›› Play Style ››

Select this option if you want Overture to play back any and all repeats in your score. When you select Repeats, Overture plays all repeats it sees in your score, including standard repeat barlines, endings, and jumps (like D.S. al Coda, or D.C. al Fine). If you do not select this option, Overture's MIDI playback ignores all repeats in a score.
