Deleting Barlines

Symbol Palettes ›› Barlines Palette ››

You can delete dotted barlines by clicking their edit handles with the Eraser Cursor. Similarly, you can convert any type of barline (except dotted) into a standard barline by clicking its edit handle with the Eraser Cursor.


The Barline edit handle is located at the top of the barline, where the barline crosses the top line in the staff.


Edit handles are always available, but normally hidden unless you select Options>Show>Handles. The edit handle does not have to be visible in order for you to click it. For more information about editing and moving barlines, see Changing Bar Widths.


Hiding Standard Barlines

You can hide standard barlines by clicking their edit handles with the Eraser Cursor.


If you choose Options>Show>Invisibles, Overture displays the hidden barlines in gray (though they still are invisible when printing).


To make a hidden barline visible again:


  1. Select the Standard Barline tool from the Barlines palette.
    The cursor turns into a crosshair when you move it over the Score View.
  2. Click the cursor close to the barline you wish to change.
    Overture changes the hidden barline into a standard visible barline.
