Articulations/Ornaments

Overture Menus ›› Notes Menu ›› Delete ››

Notes>Delete>Articulations/Ornaments


Choose this command to remove all articulations and ornaments from the selected notes. For example:


  1. Select some notes with attached articulations and ornaments.
  2. Choose Notes>Delete>Articulations/Ornaments.


All articulations and ornaments attached to the selected notes are deleted.
