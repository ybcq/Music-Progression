How do I create separate instrument parts from a complete score?


Score has automatic part extraction.


To view a part, choose the part from the Active Parts Menu at the top right onf the Control Bar.

You can Save or Print the part as normal.


or


Open the Score Layout Panel and choose the Active Parts section.


To view a part click on it's name in the parts list.

The part will appear in the Score View.


Use the Buttons at the top to Print, Save, Delete, etc. the Active Part.


NOTE: Because parts are automatically extracted there is limited to moving items around like text and expressions.

If you need to edit the part beyond this you can save the part and then open it as an individual score.


More capibilities will be added in the future.
