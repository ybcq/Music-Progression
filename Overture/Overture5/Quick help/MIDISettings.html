MIDI Settings

Audio/MIDI>MIDI Settings


Choose this command to setup your MIDI devices.


When you choose the MIDI Settings command, an MIDI Settings dialog box appears.

There are several elements in the MIDI Settings dialog box. These are described, below:


  • Input Devices - Click on the Enable/Disable field next to a MIDI device to enable/disable the MIDI device. To test the device play a note on your keyboard and the box to the right of the device should blink red.
  • Output Devices - Click on the Enable/Disable field next to a MIDI device to enable/disable the MIDI device. To test the device click on the arrow to the right of the device. You should hear a MIDI note.
  • Instrument Name - Choose the Instrument definition file that corresponds to your MIDI device. If you do not see a definition file, then use the General MIDI definition file as a default. Oveture uses Instrument Definition files when choosing instruments for tracks.


NOTE: If you do not see your MIDI device in the list, check the connection and try rebooting Overture. Overture retrieves the list of MIDI devices from the operating system.
