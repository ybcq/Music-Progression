Score Layout Panel


If the Layout panel is not showing click on the Layout button at top right of Control Bar or type "Shift-L" to open the panel.


The Layout Panel has three panels for controlling the score appearance.

Sections - allows you to choose what to edit.

  • Score - Used for setting overall appearance of the score.
  • Active Parts - Used for setting the appearance of extracted parts called "Active Parts"
  • Engraver - Used for setting spacing of elements nad line thickness
