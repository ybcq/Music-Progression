Adding a Slur

Getting Started ›› Entering symbols ››

  1. Select the slur tool from the Groups palette.
    The groups palette has a number of tools for marking multiple note, like ties, slurs, and ottava signs.
  2. Drag a rectangle around the notes that you want to group.
    When you release the mouse button, a slur appears.


To alter the shape of the slur, either drag the slur or click it to display shaping handles.


Note: If you wish to draw slurs directly, open the Preferences Dialog and check "Draw slurs while inserting".
