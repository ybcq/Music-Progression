Glissando Wavy

Overture Menus ›› Notes Menu ›› Group ››

Notes>Group>Glissando Wavy


Choose this command to connect the selected notes with a wavy line glissando. Generally, glissandos connect two neighboring notes (the highest and lowest in the glissando). If you select more than two notes, Overture creates a glissando between the first and last notes in the selection.


You can modify the angle and length of any glissando by clicking and dragging either end. You can modify the vertical or horizontal position of any glissando by clicking and dragging its middle.


To set the MIDI playback for a glissando double-click on a glissando to open the Edit Glissando dialog box.
