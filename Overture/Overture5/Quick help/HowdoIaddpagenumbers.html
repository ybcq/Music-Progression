How do I add page numbers?


Double click on either footer at the bottom of the page to open it's dialog.
Note: If there is currently no footer text you'll see [Footer] displayed in gray.


or


Open the  the Score Layout Panel and choose the Active Parts Section and then click on Footer.


You can set the text, the font, and the alignment (left, right, or center).

You can have Overture automatically add the current page number to the bottom of the page by typing a special code, #P. For example, typing the words “Page #P” in a footer makes Overture display “Page1” on the first page.


If you wish to have page number at the top of the page, repeat the above process by using Headers.
