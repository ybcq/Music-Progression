How do I record notes and rests?

Frequently Asked Questions ››

If you're a good keyboard player, Overture can make excellent transcriptions of your playing. You can build a recording one staff at a time, and even play percussion parts into Overture, with percussion notes mapped to the correct staves. Click any measure in a staff where you want to begin, click the Record button in the Control bar, and you're off!
