If Tools Panel is not showing click on the "Score Tools" button at top left of Control Bar or type "Shift-T" to open the panel and then on the Lyric section if it's not active. You can also type "Ctrl[cmd]-Shift-L" to go directly to the Lyric section.


There are two methods of entering lyrics:


Entering a entire line from the Lyric panel:

  1. Set the score position using the numerical fields at the top or click in the score below a note.
  2. Type in the entire text in the panel's text box and then press the Apply button.


Entering the lyric words or syllables directly on the score:

  1. Click in the score below a note where you want to start inserting text. A small text box appears above the note.
  2. Type in the word or syllable in the text box. Press space bar to go to next note or press return to enter the text and exit entering lyrics. Press the Esc key at any time to end entering lyrics directly on the score
