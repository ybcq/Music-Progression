How do I make the music smaller on the page?


You can change the size of any track/staff using the Track Inspector. Open the Track Inspector and choose the Settings Tab.

Click and drag in the Point Size numeric to change the tracks point size. You can also single click and type in the number.


When the Point Size is 24, Overture displays music in its usual size. When the Scale setting is smaller than 24, Overture displays the music at a smaller size--everything on the staff becomes smaller, including markings, notes, and lyrics.


For example, you’ll often want to make a vocal staff in a Piano/Vocal lead sheet smaller than the piano staves. In this case, you might set the Vocal staff to 20, leaving the piano staves at 24.

For more information on formatting your music, see See Arranging Staves and Systems


To make one staff a different size than the rest:


  1. Click the track you want to change
  2. Open the Track Inspector and choose the Settings Tab
  3. Change the Point Size setting (to 20, for example)--you can click in the number field and drag up or down to change the value


To make many staves the same size at once:


Use Overture's handy Alt[Option]-dragging shortcut to apply a change to many items at once, in this case, staves.


  1. In a score with many tracks, click the topmost track you want to change.
  2. Open the Track Inspector and choose the Settings Tab
  3. While holding the Alt[Option] key, click in the Scale setting and change the value by dragging up or down


You can also click on any track in the score to see to examine every staff in the score, applying any changes you want to each staff individually.


or


You can also right-click (Option Click) on the empty area in the panel and choose Next or Previous to examine another track.
