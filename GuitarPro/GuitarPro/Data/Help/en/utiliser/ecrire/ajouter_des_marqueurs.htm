Adding Sections

Sections are markers to allow you to easily get to any part of the score. They can be set as letters and/or names (Intro, Chorus, Verse, etc.).

The markers a tied to the bars, and are therefore linked to the master track.

 

To add sections, you can:

  • Click on the button in the Edition panel;

  • Use the menu: Bar > Sections > Insert/Remove.

 

 

You can navigate between sections by use of the shortcut keys. (Cf. Shortcut Keys.)

   Tip: You can also go directly to a given section via the Edit > Go to... menu.
