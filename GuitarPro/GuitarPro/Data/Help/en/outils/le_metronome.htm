The Metronome

To activate the Metronome, use the Sound > Metronome, or click on .

(You can also get a one-bar Countdown before playback by clicking on .)

The metronome gives you the tempo during your score's playback.

 

 

You can also use as a regular metronome for practice:

 

  1. Create a new non-empty file (menu File > New)

  2. Set the desired tempo (menu Sound > Tempo)

  3. Activate the looped play (menu Sound > Play in Loops / Speed Trainer)

  4. Play the score (menu Sound > Play from the Beginning)

 

The first (empty) bar will then be played indefinitely over, with the tick of the metronome.
