Technical Support

Before you contact our online Tech Support

  • Register your licence on the Guitar Pro website www.guitar-pro.com.

 

  • Make sure you have already installed the latest version.

 

To download the latest versions of the user's manual, language packs, and the software in general, just use the Guitar Pro Updater. (This top-left logo lets you know that an update is available for download. Simply click on it. If no update is available, the logo you see is .)  Please also make sure that what you are looking for isn't already in this manual, as it has been designed to address most of the requests coming from GP6 users. .

 

If your problems persist, or if you are having problems installing Guitar Pro, please log into the SUPPORT section of the Guitar Pro website www.guitar-pro.com.

 

Please note: The distributor of your country may offer additional technical support via the Internet, telephone or postal mail. In that case, a document will be included in the software’s package, with instructions to access that support.

Please note: Technical support for any given version of Guitar Pro is guaranteed for one full year after the end of the distribution of this version.
